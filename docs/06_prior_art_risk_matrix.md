# Prior-Art Risk Matrix

Status: G1 working document; candidate novelty audit only.

Purpose: convert the G1 literature map and novelty framing into a reviewer-risk matrix. This file identifies what the nearest prior art already covers, what the current project may still add, and what evidence is required before any claim can be promoted.

Boundary: this file does not validate any model, equation, numerical result, or manuscript claim.

## Summary judgment

The project has a possible novelty window, but only if framed narrowly:

> The candidate novelty is not hydrogel oscillation itself. It is a material-internal, LCST-gated transport-barrier route by which a single non-oscillatory exothermic reaction may become a spatial relaxation oscillator.

The strongest prior-art collisions are:

1. BZ self-oscillating gels and Balazs-group BZ gel models.
2. He/Aizenberg/Balazs SMARTS homeostatic materials.
3. Ximin He group self-sustained hydrogel oscillator / phototactic swimmer.
4. Zhang/Ikkala feedback-controlled thermoresponsive hydrogel oscillations.
5. Responsive-gel oscillations driven by non-oscillatory reactions.
6. Autocatalytic reaction-diffusion front hydrogel actuators.
7. Photothermal self-shadowing robots and light-driven thermoresponsive gels.

## Nearest-prior-art family details

### Family F1. BZ self-oscillating gels and Balazs-group BZ models

Risk:

* Blocks broad claims that hydrogels or polymer gels can self-oscillate.
* Provides established reaction-diffusion-mechanics gel modeling language.

Project survival condition:

* The chemical subsystem must be explicitly non-oscillatory.
* The oscillation must disappear or change qualitatively when LCST transport/accessibility feedback is removed.

Manuscript implication:

* Cite BZ gels early as the established class.
* Frame this project as a different route to oscillation, not the first gel oscillator.

### Family F2. He/Aizenberg/Balazs SMARTS homeostatic materials

Risk:

* Very close because it uses exothermic catalytic chemistry, thermoresponsive gel mechanics, and homeostatic regulation.

Project survival condition:

* Show that the proposed feedback switch is not a catalyst-bearing microstructure moving into/out of a nutrient layer.
* Emphasize continuous-medium internal transport/accessibility barrier.

Manuscript implication:

* Do not use `homeostatic hydrogel` without explaining the distinction.
* Use `material-internal transport barrier` rather than `chemo-mechano-chemical homeostasis` as the central phrase.

### Family F3. Ximin He group self-sustained hydrogel oscillator and phototactic swimmer

Risk:

* Blocks broad phrases such as `self-sustained hydrogel oscillator` as novelty.

Project survival condition:

* Distinguish chemical heat source and internal transport barrier from photothermal/soft-robot actuation cycles.

Manuscript implication:

* Position He-group work as an important adjacent oscillator platform.
* Do not imply that hydrogel self-oscillation itself is new.

### Family F4. Zhang/Ikkala feedback-controlled thermoresponsive hydrogels

Risk:

* Very close LCST/PNIPAm delayed-feedback oscillator precedent.

Project survival condition:

* Distinguish external light/transmittance switching from reaction-generated heat and reactant transport shutdown.

Manuscript implication:

* Explicitly contrast optical transmittance feedback with chemical reactant-access feedback.

### Family F5. Responsive-gel oscillations from non-oscillatory reactions

Risk:

* Potentially the most direct phrase-level collision with `non-oscillatory reaction induces oscillation`.

Project survival condition:

* Establish that the specific novelty is LCST shell/barrier and surface/bulk spatial slow manifold, not simply non-oscillatory chemistry plus gel.

Manuscript implication:

* Avoid claiming that non-oscillatory chemistry plus responsive gel is new.

### Family F6. Autocatalytic front hydrogel actuators and RD meta-gels

Risk:

* Shows reaction-diffusion fronts can organize hydrogel actuation and spatiotemporal response.

Project survival condition:

* Demonstrate sustained recurrent cycle rather than one-shot front, excitable front, or relay-like actuation.

Manuscript implication:

* Use `front-mediated` carefully. If using it, add `relaxation cycle` and `transport barrier`.

### Family F7. Photothermal self-shadowing robots and light-driven soft oscillators

Risk:

* Blocks claims about converting constant input into oscillatory hydrogel motion.

Project survival condition:

* Distinguish external optical geometry/self-shadowing from internal reaction-transport-phase-transition feedback.

Manuscript implication:

* Do not present optical systems as weak prior art; they are strong in applications but mechanistically distinct.

## Allowed claim templates

Use only with `candidate`, `we investigate`, or `model suggests` until evidence is generated.

1. `We investigate whether LCST collapse can provide a material-internal transport gate that converts a non-oscillatory exothermic reaction into a relaxation oscillator.`
2. `The candidate mechanism differs from BZ gels because the chemistry is not assumed to be intrinsically oscillatory.`
3. `The candidate mechanism differs from optical self-shadowing systems because the feedback switch is a collapsed transport barrier rather than a modulated light path.`
4. `The candidate mechanism differs from microstructure-switched homeostatic materials because the reaction gate is formed by a continuous collapsed shell rather than by discrete catalyst-bearing structures.`
5. `The model will be judged by whether transport-barrier controls, homogeneous limits, and spatial diagnostics support this mechanism.`

## Prohibited or high-risk claim templates

Do not use before strong evidence and prior-art clearance.

1. `We discover the first self-oscillating hydrogel.`
2. `This is the first autonomous hydrogel oscillator.`
3. `This is a new class of self-oscillating gels.`
4. `A BZ-free oscillator is new.`
5. `Linear stability explains the oscillation.`
6. `The transport barrier is validated.`
7. `The LCST-front cycle is experimentally established.`
8. `The mechanism is universal for thermoresponsive gels.`

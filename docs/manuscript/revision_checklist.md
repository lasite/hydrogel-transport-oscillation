# Revision Checklist

Status: pre-manuscript

## Scientific risks

* Is the mechanism new?
* Is the model overfitted?
* Is oscillation numerical artifact?
* Is the well-mixed limit actually oscillatory?
* Is the LCST collapse law physically justified?
* Are boundary conditions driving the oscillation?
* Are claims stronger than evidence?

## Required before manuscript

* Literature matrix populated.
* Model spec validated.
* At least one reduced diagnostic proving barrier formation.
* Grid and timestep convergence.
* Control simulations:

  * no collapse;
  * no transport degradation;
  * no reaction accessibility feedback;
  * homogeneous ODE.

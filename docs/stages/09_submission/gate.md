# Gate: 09 Submission

## Status

blocked

## Purpose

Prevent premature submission before manuscript, evidence, figures, and reproducibility checks are complete.

## Required outputs

* Final manuscript PDF.
* Final supplement PDF.
* Complete bibliography.
* Final figure files.
* Reproducibility statement or archive plan.
* Venue-specific checklist.

## Acceptance criteria

* All submission files compile or render.
* References are complete.
* Claims match validated evidence.
* Venue requirements are checked.

## Evidence

* Paper files are placeholders.
* No reviewed manuscript exists.

## Blockers

* Review gate blocked.
* Writing, figures, and evidence gates are not complete.

## Next tasks

* Begin only after internal review and final revisions are complete.

## Promotion rule

Submit only after the human lead approves the final package.

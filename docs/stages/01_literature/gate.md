# Gate: G1 Literature / Novelty Mapping

## Status

active

## Purpose

Prevent manuscript and novelty claims from advancing before the relevant literature has been surveyed and neighboring mechanisms have been separated from the project's candidate mechanism.

G1 maps literature and novelty risk. It does not perform model equation review, numerical verification, figure generation, or manuscript drafting.

## Required outputs

* Populated `docs/04_literature_map.md`.
* Literature matrix with traceable sources.
* Mechanistic comparison to BZ gels, LCST hydrogels, thermal runaway fronts, thermochemical oscillators, transport-limited gels, and self-oscillating polymer systems.
* Clear list of novelty risks.
* Provisional novelty framing that remains separate from validated claims.

## Acceptance criteria

* Each matrix entry has a real source.
* Similar and competing mechanisms are separated from this project's candidate mechanism.
* Novelty claims are phrased as provisional until evidence is recorded.
* No model, derivation, numerical result, or manuscript claim is promoted to validated status.
* The next gate is explicitly chosen after literature review.
